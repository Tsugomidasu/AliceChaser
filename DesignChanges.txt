Alice Chaser - Design Changes

- Alice always faces right during gameplay. Moving left does not turn her around, so her melee attacks remain directed to the right.
- The charged heavy attack reuses the third combo attack animation instead of having a separate heavy attack animation.
- Custom visual effects were added to Alice's attacks to provide clearer combat feedback.
- Alice can perform a basic attack while airborne, allowing her to fight jumping and flying enemies.
- Enemy projectiles can be struck by Alice's attacks and reflected toward enemies, allowing reflected projectiles to damage them.
